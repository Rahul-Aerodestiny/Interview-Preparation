📘 Basic Mechanical Interview Questions

Core questions and answers for Mechanical Engineering interviews.

1. What is the difference between a 2-stroke and 4-stroke engine?

Ans: 2-stroke completes a power cycle in 2 strokes, lighter but less efficient; 4-stroke requires 4 strokes, more efficient and durable.

2. Explain the laws of thermodynamics.

Ans: Zeroth: defines temperature, First: conservation of energy, Second: entropy, Third: absolute zero.

3. What is the difference between stress and strain?

Ans: Stress = load/area, Strain = deformation/original length.

4. What is creep in materials?

Ans: Time-dependent deformation under constant load at high temperature.

5. Explain heat treatment processes.

Ans: Annealing, quenching, tempering used to change mechanical properties of metals.

6. What are the types of fits?

Ans: Clearance fit, interference fit, transition fit.

7. Explain the difference between NC, CNC, and DNC machines.

Ans: NC = Numerical control, CNC = Computer numerical control, DNC = Distributed numerical control with network.

8. What is the purpose of a flywheel?

Ans: Stores rotational energy to smoothen fluctuations in engine speed.

9. What is a governor in mechanical systems?

Ans: Device to regulate engine speed automatically by adjusting fuel supply.

10. Difference between welding, brazing, and soldering?

Ans: Welding melts base metal, brazing uses filler at >450°C, soldering uses filler <450°C.

11. What is a Universal Testing Machine (UTM)?

Ans: Machine used to test tensile, compression, and bending strength of materials.

12. What is the function of a clutch?

Ans: To engage/disengage power transmission between engine and gearbox.

13. Difference between petrol and diesel engines?

Ans: Petrol: spark ignition, lightweight, high RPM; Diesel: compression ignition, fuel-efficient, more torque.

14. What is CAD/CAE?

Ans: CAD = Computer-Aided Design, CAE = Computer-Aided Engineering (simulation, FEA, CFD).

15. Explain the Rankine cycle.

Ans: Thermodynamic cycle for steam power plants involving pump, boiler, turbine, and condenser.
